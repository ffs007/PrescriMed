# Prototype extension rules
- Legacy catalog items absent from the prototype live in legacy-data.js (generated once, origem base_anterior). Why: extend the prototype without editing its files.
- PDF styling is applied by lovable-docstyle.js wrapping jsPDF (scales page content into margins, draws logo/header/footer; official forms skipped by default). Why: style every PDF without editing prototype generators.
- Expanded catalog content lives in expansion-data.js / scores-extra.js (origem expansao_ia, always flagged "confira a dose"); custom doctor conditions and the blank prescription are injected by lovable-custom.js before app.js. Why: grow the base additively in the prototype's own format.
- Medication safety (renal ClCr, pregnancy/lactation gates, calculators, hospital list additions) lives in lovable-safety.js / lovable-decision.js / hosp-expansion.js with rule tables renal-data.js and preg-data.js; overrides and alerts go append-only to safety_events through __psxBridge.logSafety. Why: one auditable safety layer over the unmodified prototype.
- Pediatric anamnesis/exam blocks live in peds-anam.js (Proxy over CLIN maps, before app.js); nursing check sheet (aprazamento) in lovable-nursing.js; trimester-specific pregnancy categories via tri/sem fields in preg-data.js. Why: additive over the unmodified prototype.
- Patient-field sanity warnings (age, weight, creatinine, height, GA) live in lovable-validate.js; same-name conditions are merged in lovable-custom.js (prototype original wins over leg_*). Why: safety and dedup without editing prototype files.
- Contextual score recommendation side panel, pediatric calculators and arrest card live in lovable-cdss.js (loaded last); records go to safety_events via PSX_SAFE.log (tipo registro_escore/registro_calculo). Why: one persistent decision-support layer over the unmodified prototype.
- Emergency mode (full-screen 3-block flow, shortcuts, timer, post-review queue) lives in lovable-emergency.js; prescriptions reuse the compatibility template API on the PrescriMed condition and log emergency review events. Why: speed without bypassing dose, allergy and review gates.
- Emergency critical-care tools (PCR console with timers/metronome, Box de Emergência calculators and guides) live in lovable-emergency-box.js, loaded after lovable-emergency.js; records go to safety_events via PSX_SAFE.log. Why: additive over the unmodified prototype and emergency mode.
- Document completion and module ordering live in lovable-documents.js (after app.js); the ambulatory and discharge prescription are one module using C.rx. Why: one prescription flow on the artifact engine.
- The ambulatory prescription PDF is unified by lovable-rxunified.js (after lovable-documents.js): it wraps RxPDF.build to paginate common items 10 per portrait page with continuous numbering and appends CtrlPDF pages as landscape sheets with both copies side by side, in one PDF. Why: one emission flow without editing the prototype generators.
- The automated discharge report (condition library, "O que foi visto hoje" aggregation, special contexts, personal blocks, versions, review gate) lives in lovable-alta.js after lovable-rxunified.js; emission logs emissao_documento via PSX_SAFE.log. Why: additive over the unmodified prototype.
