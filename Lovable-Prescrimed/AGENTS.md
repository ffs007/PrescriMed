- The authenticated app at /app is the PrescriMed clinical artifact served from public/prototype/ in an iframe; its AI bridge remains compatible with the existing backend function. Why: the artifact is the source of truth for the clinical engine and PDFs.
- Enhancements to the prototype are extra scripts loaded after app.js (e.g. lovable-search.js) that hook the DOM; prototype files stay byte-identical. Why: keep upstream artifact replaceable.
- The unit config (localStorage psx.cfg) is mirrored to profiles.psx_cfg by PrototypePage. Why: settings follow the doctor's account across devices.
- psx.docstyle and psx.custom localStorage keys are mirrored to profiles.psx_docstyle / psx_custom by PrototypePage. Why: per-doctor, cross-device.
- Personal prescription templates live in public/prototype/lovable-templates.js (loaded after app.js) and persist via __psxBridge.tpl → tpl_* tables (versions immutable, access via fn_tpl_nivel RLS); applying a template re-selects drugs so the prototype engine recalculates doses. Why: reuse clinical knowledge without forking the prototype or storing stale doses.
- Clinical protocols in three layers (pessoal/equipe/institucional) live in public/prototype/lovable-protocols.js (loaded after lovable-templates.js) via __psxBridge.prot → prot_* tables; access by fn_prot_nivel RLS, approvals/refusal rules enforced by triggers; institutional base copied once from PROTO_DB.protocols; applying reuses PSX_TPL.applyTemplate({track:false}). Why: one governed protocol layer over the unmodified prototype engine.
